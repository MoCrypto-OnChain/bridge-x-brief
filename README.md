## Weekly publishing workflow

1. Research the subject using primary and authoritative sources.
2. Draft the LinkedIn article and the full branded web edition.
3. Validate links, dates, legal status, and factual distinctions.
4. Add the new edition under `editions/`.
5. Add its card to the archive homepage.
6. Commit and publish.
7. Share the permanent GitHub Pages link through LinkedIn and other channels.
