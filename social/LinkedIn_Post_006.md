Your bank deposit is going on-chain.

Wells Fargo announced that it will begin rolling out tokenized deposits for select corporate and commercial clients this fall—starting with U.S. dollar-to-British pound transactions and expanding through 2027.

The important distinction: this is not a bank launching a conventional stablecoin. The customer’s money remains a bank deposit. What changes is the operating layer underneath it—opening the door to 24/7 settlement, conditional payments and more programmable treasury workflows.
